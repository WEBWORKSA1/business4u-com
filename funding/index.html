How to choose the right funding

  • Loans keep you in full ownership but must be repaid, usually from cash flow. Best when you have revenue or a clear path to it.
  • Grants don't need repaying but are competitive and often limited to specific sectors, locations or founder groups.
  • Accelerators and equity trade ownership for capital and support. Best for businesses aiming for fast, large growth.
  • Crowdfunding doubles as market validation: if people won't back it, that's useful information.
  • Mentorship programs are free and often unlock loans and grants through introductions.
