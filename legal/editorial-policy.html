Editorial policy

How we decide what to publish, how we check it, and how we keep it honest.

Our standard

Every page must help a real owner make a decision or take an action. We write in plain language, show our formulas and worked examples, and link to official sources so readers can check us.

How content is made

  1. Research starts with primary sources: government agencies, tax authorities, official program pages and regulators.
  2. Drafting is done by our editorial team. We use software tools, including AI writing assistants, to help research, outline and check drafts.
  3. Review: an editor checks every page for accuracy, clarity and usefulness before it's published. Calculators are tested against hand-worked examples.
  4. Updates: guides are reviewed at least once a year and whenever a major rule or program changes. Each guide shows its last update date.

Independence from advertisers

Advertising is served automatically by Google and is not chosen by our editors. Sponsors may fund a tool, series or prize, but they never approve, edit or rank our content, and sponsored placements are always labelled. Referral fees from Get Matched partners never affect what we write or which programs appear in the funding finder.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it promptly and, for material errors, note the correction on the page. Report an error through our contact page and choose "Content correction".

Expert contributors

Members of our expert network may review guides or provide quotes. When they do, we name them and their credentials and note any commercial relationship.
