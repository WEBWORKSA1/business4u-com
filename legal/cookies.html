Cookie policy

Last updated November 12, 2026.

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature. This page explains what Business4U.com uses and why.

TypeSet byPurpose
Preferences (local storage)Business4U.comRemembers your theme, currency, saved names, invoice and plan drafts, and that you dismissed the cookie notice. Stays on your device.
Advertising cookiesGoogle and its partnersServe and measure ads, limit repetition and, where permitted, personalise ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
Consent cookiesGoogle's consent platformStore your advertising choices if you're in the EEA, UK or Switzerland.
Embedded videoYouTube (Google)Set only after you press play on a video.

Managing cookies

  • Personalised ads: Google Ads Settings, aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies and local storage. Blocking them may stop some features, such as saved drafts, from working.
