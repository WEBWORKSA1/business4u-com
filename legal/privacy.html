Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026.

This policy explains what information Business4U.com ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (Get Matched, contact, newsletter, contest entry, application, pledge or advertising enquiry), we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, business details and message. Forms are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service, which processes the data on our behalf.

Information collected automatically

  • Your preferences (theme, currency, saved names, invoice and plan drafts) are stored in your own browser using local storage. They are not sent to us.
  • Hosting logs. Our host may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested to keep the service secure and running.
  • Advertising cookies, described below.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform before personalised ads or related cookies are used.

Embedded content and third-party services

  • YouTube videos load only when you press play and use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). After you press play, Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Google Fonts are loaded from Google's servers to display our typefaces.
  • Payment providers (such as PayPal or Stripe) process contributions on their own sites under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your card details.
  • Links to other websites are not covered by this policy.

How we use information

  • To respond to your requests and messages.
  • For Get Matched: to review your request and, with your consent, introduce you to up to three relevant providers, who may contact you by the method you chose. Providers receive only the details needed for your request.
  • To run contests, evaluate applications and process sponsorships.
  • To send the newsletter if you subscribed. Every issue includes an unsubscribe option.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent fraud and comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information. Some partners may pay us a referral fee when you choose to work with them; this does not change how your data is handled.

Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you submitted them, typically up to 24 months, unless the law requires longer. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR and UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your information, to withdraw consent, to object to certain processing and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. To make a request, use our contact page and choose "Privacy or data request". We will respond within the time required by law. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. Contest entrants must be 18 or older.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so please don't submit sensitive information such as government ID or bank account numbers through our forms.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the contact page.
