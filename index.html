Questions owners ask us

Is Business4U.com really free?

Yes. Every tool, guide and video is free to use without an account. We earn money from ads served by Google, sponsorships, and referral fees when a partner you choose to work with pays us. That never changes the price you pay.

How does Get Matched work?

You tell us what you need, your stage and your timeline. We review it and introduce you to providers that fit, such as lenders, formation services, accountants, insurers or marketers. You decide whether to work with anyone.

Will checking funding options affect my credit?

No. Our questions use ranges you choose yourself. A lender may run a credit check later only if you decide to apply with them.

Is this legal or financial advice?

No. Our content is general information to help you make better decisions. For advice on your situation, speak with a licensed professional. You can find one through Get Matched.
