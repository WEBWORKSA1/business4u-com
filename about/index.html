About Business4U.com

Business, built around you. We make the first years of owning a business less lonely, less confusing and less expensive.

What we do

Most people who start a business are experts in their craft, not in loans, tax registration or pricing models. Business4U.com puts the practical side in one place: calculators that do the maths, guides that explain the steps in plain language, a directory of real funding programs, curated video lessons, and a free way to get matched with professionals when you need one.

What we believe

  • Useful beats impressive. Every page should help you make a decision or take an action today.
  • Free should mean free. No sign-up walls on tools or guides.
  • Numbers before opinions. We show formulas and worked examples so you can check our reasoning.
  • Honesty about money. We tell you how we earn it (below), and it never changes what we recommend.

How we make money

Business4U.com is free to use. We earn revenue from advertising served by Google AdSense, from clearly labelled sponsorships, from referral fees paid by some partners when an owner chooses to work with them through Get Matched, and from voluntary contributions by supporters. Our editorial policy explains how we keep these separate from our content.

Who we are

Business4U.com is an independently owned website run by a small remote team of editors, developers and contributors, supported by a growing network of accountants, lawyers, lenders and marketers who review and contribute to our guides. We're hiring writers and creators.
