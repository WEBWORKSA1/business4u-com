Official rules

1. Sponsor and contest type

The Founder Pitch of the Month ("Contest") is organised by Business4U.com ("Sponsor"). It is a contest of skill. Winners are chosen by judges using the published criteria; chance plays no part. No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win, and a purchase does not improve your chances.

2. Eligibility

Open to individuals who are at least 18 years old and have reached the age of majority in their place of residence. Employees of the Sponsor, its judges and prize sponsors, and their immediate families, are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

3. Entry period and how to enter

Each Contest runs from the first day to the last day of a calendar month, ending at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time. Enter by submitting the form on this page. Limit one entry per person and per business per month. Entries must be original, truthful and owned by the entrant, and must not infringe anyone's rights or contain unlawful or offensive content. Incomplete or automated entries are void.

4. Judging

Editors select up to ten finalists. A panel scores finalists on clarity (30%), customer and market (25%), feasibility (25%) and originality (20%). In a tie, the highest clarity score wins. Judges' decisions are final.

5. Prizes

The prize for each month is the one shown on this page when that month's Contest opens. It always includes a featured spotlight on Business4U.com, and may include cash or sponsor-provided prizes when a sponsor funds the month. Prizes are not transferable and no substitution is allowed except by the Sponsor, which may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes.

6. Winner notification

Winners are notified by email within 14 days after the month closes and must reply within 7 days, or an alternate winner may be selected. Canadian residents selected as winners may be required to correctly answer a time-limited skill-testing question. Winners may be asked to sign a declaration of eligibility and publicity release where permitted by law.

7. Publicity and privacy

By entering, you agree that your name, business name, location and pitch may be published if you are a finalist or winner, except where prohibited by law. Personal information is handled under our privacy policy and used only to run the Contest and, if you opt in, to send updates.

8. Quebec residents

Any litigation respecting the conduct or organisation of a publicity contest may be submitted to the Régie des alcools, des courses et des jeux for a ruling. Any litigation respecting the awarding of a prize may be submitted to the Régie only for the purpose of helping the parties reach a settlement.

9. General

The Sponsor may cancel, suspend or modify the Contest if fraud, technical failure or any other factor impairs its integrity, and may disqualify anyone who tampers with the entry process. These rules are governed by the laws applicable in the Sponsor's place of business. Questions: use our contact page.
