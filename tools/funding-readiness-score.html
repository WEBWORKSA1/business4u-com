What lenders actually look at

Every lender has its own model, but most weigh the same handful of factors, sometimes summarised as the "five Cs": character (credit history), capacity (cash flow to repay), capital (what you have invested), collateral (assets that secure the loan) and conditions (the purpose and the economy). This score turns those into ranges you can check in two minutes.
