Line items

What every invoice should include

  • Your business name and contact details, plus your tax registration number where required.
  • A unique invoice number. Sequential numbers make bookkeeping and audits simple.
  • Issue date and a clear due date.
  • The client's name and address.
  • Itemised products or services with quantity, rate and amount.
  • Subtotal, discounts, tax shown separately, and the total due.
  • Payment terms and how to pay.

Get paid faster

  1. Invoice the same day you finish the work.
  2. Use short terms, such as 14 days, and state them clearly.
  3. Offer an easy payment method such as bank transfer or a card payment link.
  4. For larger projects, ask for a deposit of 30% to 50% upfront.
  5. Send a polite reminder the day after the due date, then weekly.
