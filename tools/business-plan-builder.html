One-page business plan builder

Answer the prompts in plain language. The plan on the right builds as you type and calculates your key numbers. Print it, save as PDF, or copy it into any document.

Why one page is enough to start

A long plan is useful when a bank or investor asks for one. Before that, a single page forces clarity: who the customer is, why they will pay, how they will find you, and whether the numbers work. Update it monthly as you learn. When you need the full version, our guide on how to write a business plan shows how to expand each section.

Tips for each section

  • Problem: describe it in the customer's words, not yours.
  • Customer: narrow beats broad. "Parents of under-5s within 3 km" is better than "families".
  • Revenue: state prices. A plan without prices is a wish.
  • Milestones: make them measurable, such as "20 paying customers by March 31".
