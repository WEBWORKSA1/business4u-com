Business valuation calculator

Get a realistic first estimate of what an owner-operated business could sell for, the way buyers and brokers usually start the conversation.

How small businesses are usually valued

Larger companies are often valued on EBITDA. Smaller owner-run businesses are usually valued on seller's discretionary earnings (SDE): the total financial benefit to one full-time owner. Start with net profit, then add back the owner's salary and perks, interest, depreciation and one-off expenses that a new owner wouldn't face.

The SDE is multiplied by a factor that reflects risk and quality. A revenue multiple is a rough cross-check, useful when profits are temporarily low.

What moves the multiple up or down

Pushes value upPushes value down
Recurring or contracted revenueOne or two customers make up most sales
Documented processes and a team that runs day to dayOwner is the business
Three or more years of clean, growing financialsMessy or cash-heavy books
Transferable lease, licences and supplier contractsShort lease or key licences that can't transfer

Worked example

A local service company has $650,000 of revenue and $160,000 of SDE. At 2x to 3.5x SDE, plus $25,000 of inventory and equipment, the range is $345,000 to $585,000. A 0.6x revenue cross-check gives $415,000, so a blended estimate lands around $440,000.

This is an estimate, not an appraisal. For a sale, loan or partner buyout, get a formal valuation from a qualified business appraiser or broker.

Frequently asked questions

Which multiple should I use?

Look at recent sales of similar businesses in your industry and region; brokers and listing sites publish ranges. Owner-operated businesses commonly sit somewhere around 1.5x to 4x SDE.

Is inventory included in the multiple?

Often not. Many deals add inventory at cost on top of the price, which is why it is a separate input here.

How can I raise my valuation before selling?

Clean up the books, reduce dependence on yourself and on any single customer, and lock in recurring revenue 12 to 24 months before you sell.
