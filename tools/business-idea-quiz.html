Business Match: which business fits you?

Six questions, about two minutes. You'll get three realistic ideas matched to your budget, time, strengths and goals, with typical startup costs and where to go next.

How the matching works

Each answer adds weight to business models that suit it. A small budget favours service businesses you can start with skills and a phone; a preference for scale favours online models that grow without adding hours. Your top three are the models that fit the most answers. The quiz is a starting point for thinking, not a promise of success: every idea still needs customers who will pay.

After you get your matches

  1. Talk to five people who would buy it. Ask what they use today and what it costs them.
  2. Run the numbers with the startup cost and break-even calculators.
  3. Find a name with the name generator.
  4. Write a one-page plan and set a 90-day goal.
