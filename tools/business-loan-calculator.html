Business loan calculator

See the monthly payment, what the loan really costs once fees are included, and how every payment splits between interest and principal.

How the payment is calculated

This calculator assumes a standard amortizing term loan with fixed monthly payments. The payment is P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (APR ÷ 12) and n is the number of months. Early payments are mostly interest; later payments are mostly principal, which you can see in the schedule.

Why "true APR" matters

Many lenders deduct an origination fee from the money you receive. On a $50,000 loan with a 2% fee you receive $49,000 but repay the full $50,000 plus interest. The calculator solves for the rate that links the cash you actually receive to the payments you make, so you can compare offers with different fees on equal terms.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good business loan rate?

It moves with central bank rates, your credit and time in business. Compare at least three offers using true APR, not the headline rate.

Does this work for lines of credit or merchant cash advances?

Not directly. Lines of credit charge interest only on what you draw, and cash advances use factor rates. Ask the provider for an APR equivalent and enter it here.

How much can I afford to borrow?

A common rule of thumb is to keep total debt payments under roughly a third of monthly operating cash flow. Check your runway with the payment included.
